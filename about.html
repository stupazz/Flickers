About•

Science Flickers is an independent editorial project exploring science fiction as a way to read the present and imagine what comes next.

Through printed fanzines, essays, illustrations, 3D experiments and visual research, the project moves between speculative storytelling and the technologies, ideas and anxieties shaping contemporary life.

We are interested in futures that feel strange but possible: artificial life, altered realities, unfamiliar ecologies, machines, signals and the unstable border between fiction and fact.

Science Flickers is a work in progress. It is a space for independent publishing, visual experimentation and stories that flicker at the edge of what we know.
